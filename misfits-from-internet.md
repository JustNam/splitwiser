**Monetization / Pricing**
- Daily expense-entry limits on the free tier push users toward paid plans

**UI / Navigation**
- Web app looks dated, wastes screen space on tablet/desktop
- Secondary features (groups, friends) are hard to find - overcrowded navigation

**Core Feature Friction**
- People pay each other in cash in real life, then forget to hit "Settle up" in the app - the digital record drifts from reality
- Rigid expense-splitting rules create social awkwardness/tension when they don't match how a group actually wants to split something
- The whole product assumes everyone in the group is willing to adopt a new tool - breaks down when even one person won't install it
